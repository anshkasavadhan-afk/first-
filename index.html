The House

A quiet floor above
a loud, brilliant street.

Grants Building has stood on P.J. Ramchandani Marg for generations, opposite the Radio Club, in the pocket of Colaba where Mumbai's colonial-era stone meets its contemporary art scene. Hotel Grant House occupies the first floor of the annexe — nine air-conditioned rooms, a front desk that's staffed around the clock, and a location that puts almost everything worth seeing within walking distance.

This isn't a resort. It's a straightforward, well-kept address for people who'd rather spend their trip at the Gateway, in a gallery, or on Colaba Causeway than in a hotel lobby.
